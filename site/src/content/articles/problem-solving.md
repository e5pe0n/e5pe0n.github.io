## what is a problem

- > A PROBLEM IS A DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THINGS AS DESIRED AND THINGS AS PERCEIVED.
  - who does desire the things? who does perceive the things?
    - naturally from the definition, who has the problem is an attribute fo the problem closely relates to the solution

## before trying to solve a problem

- judge whether you should solve the problem right now
  - it is most efficient if the problem disappears with no action
  - just partial solution or indirection action may enough to resolve the problem
    - especially if the problem is not yours. let those who have the problem solve them

## what is a problem solving

- if you are taking to solve the problem over 10 minutes, you are not thinking, just wondering
  - you don't have enough information to solve it yet, or it is just a problem of your feeling
  - to do at that time is not to stick to thinking
    - gather more information
      - research
      - get help from someone
    - refresh your mind (a problem may eventually disappear)
      - walk outside to clear your mind
      - talk with someone
      - sleep

- how to confirm that our problem definition and the solution are correct?
  - > YOU CAN NEVER BE SURE YOU HAVE A CORRECT DEFINITION, BUT DON'T EVER STOP TRYING TO GET ONE.
  - > EACH SOLUTION IS THE SOURCE OF THE NEXT PROBLEM
  - context of a problem changes over time and there may exist many things found later
    - so don't blame past decisions
  - problem solving is to run a feed back loop to decide how to take an action to a problem with clues we have at that time, act against it, evaluate the result and go to next decision adding insights from the result to the clues

## how to solve the problem

- observe the problem
  - find constraints
    - > HOW CLOUD WE CHANGE THE PROBLEM STATEMENT TO MAKE THE SOLUTION DIFFERENT?
    - conversely how cloud we change the problem statement keeping the solution?
      - which is the best problem statement
        - precision of the statement
        - help understand what is a problem
      - which is the best solution for the problem
  - > IF YOU CAN'T THINK OF AT LEAST THREE THINGS THAT MIGHT BE WRONG WITH YOUR UNDERSTANDING OF THE PROBLEM, YOU DON'T UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM.
    - view the problem at broad perspective
    - what side effect could happen as the result
